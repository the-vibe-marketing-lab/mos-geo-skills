LLM share buttons for your GEO page

A row of buttons that opens ChatGPT, Claude or Perplexity with the page summarised for the reader. This guide covers what they do and do not achieve.

What the buttons actually do

Each button opens an assistant with a prefilled prompt. The ChatGPT button uses chatgpt.com/?q= to prefill the composer, and the reader still has to press send.

Do they raise citations?

No published evidence shows that clicking a share button changes whether an engine cites the page later. Treat this as a UX nicety, not a citation mechanism.

What is not true

Buttons do not retrain a model, do not change search rankings and are not a citation mechanism. See the evidence file for sources.

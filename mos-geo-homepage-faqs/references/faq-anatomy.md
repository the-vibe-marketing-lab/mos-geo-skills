# Answer anatomy and voice

The observed Steve Toth GPT runs varied in sentence count. Neither establishes an ideal
AI-search format. Answer a real decision accurately, with enough context to stand alone.

1. Answer directly in the opening sentence. A refund question needs the actual policy,
   not a statement about how the brand helps customers succeed.
2. Name the brand/product in the opening paragraph; it need not be the first word.
   Yes/No and We/Our are allowed when appropriate to the brand voice.
3. Follow supplied brand guidelines/TOV for pronouns, vocabulary, rhythm, warmth, humour,
   formality and locale. Save the voice brief; review against real writing examples.
   Mechanical lint cannot assess tone. Voice cannot override factual support.
4. Use the detail needed: one sentence, several sentences, paragraphs, a bulleted or
   numbered list, or a small Markdown table. Separate blocks with blank lines.
   Avoid unsupported formatting and raw HTML (which the renderer escapes).
5. Preserve material qualifications: free/paid, included/add-on, current/future, licensing,
   prerequisites and limits. Never turn a roadmap item into an available feature.
6. Review a supporting passage for every factual clause or record explicit client approval.
   Question and voice files are private context, not corroboration. Missing answers stay
   pending in the question plan; never fill a policy gap with an invented answer.
7. Include dated pricing/policy facts when requested and approved, with the volatile-fact
   opt-in. No invented superlatives or promises of rankings/citations.
8. Use at most one verified internal link with words already in the answer as the anchor.

## Example (shape only; never reuse its facts)

### Do I need coding experience to use Example Studio?
No. Example Studio includes guided templates for beginners without coding experience.

You will need:

- Access to the tools named in the setup guide.
- Time to configure and test your workflow.

Each factual clause needs evidence. A conversational brand may prefer "we" and "you";
a formal one may prefer third person. No universal word range or sentence count applies.

## Placement and limits

Route specific questions to the product/service/course page where buyers need answers.
Use the homepage for broad joining decisions. A missing useful page is a content gap;
do not publish schema for a nonexistent page. Visible answers and JSON-LD must match.
No count/category quota should remove supplied Dealbreakers. Unknown answers remain in
`review/placement-plan.md` but absent from website markup.

No causal citation uplift is demonstrated by this format. FAQ blocks are ordinary
retrievable text, links support navigation/discovery, and schema is not a rich-result
promise. See the pack's `_shared/geo-evidence.md` for evidence limits.

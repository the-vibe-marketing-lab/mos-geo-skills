## The Vibe Marketing Lab Background

The Vibe Marketing Lab is a Sydney-based AI marketing education community and experimental marketing lab created by Richard Magallanes.

The platform originally developed from Richard's work documenting systems he was building with artificial intelligence and Claude Code. It subsequently narrowed its focus toward SEO, Generative Engine Optimization, AI search visibility, and practical marketing systems.

Richard works professionally in enterprise SEO and GEO while also applying similar systems to smaller freelance client accounts. The Vibe Marketing Lab is designed to document what is being tested across these environments and turn those experiments into repeatable workflows that other marketers can implement.

The central focus of The Vibe Marketing Lab is helping brands become easier for AI systems to understand, retrieve, mention, and cite.

Rather than positioning AI as a replacement for marketers, the Lab promotes a model in which AI handles repetitive research, production, analysis, and workflow tasks while humans retain responsibility for strategy, judgement, review, and execution.

The Lab also follows a build-in-public philosophy. Experiments, workflows, successes, failures, systems, and marketing results are documented so members can observe how AI-assisted SEO and GEO strategies develop over time.
